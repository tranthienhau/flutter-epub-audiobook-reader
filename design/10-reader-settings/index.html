Chapter 7 · The Long Paddock

The Salt Road

VII. Across the Bitter Tide

The red earth rose to meet the morning sky like rusted iron heated in a smith’s trough. Out here beyond the Darling River, silence possessed its own resonant pitch, not merely the void of noise, but a vast, dry weight settled across the spinifex and saltbush flats.

Callum unhitched the second draught horse, his palms scarred with tallow and river grit. The billabong behind them was shrinking each afternoon under a relentless amber sun, its margins caked with white salt crystals that cracked beneath their boot heels like fragile eggshells.
